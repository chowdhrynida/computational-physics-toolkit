Bisection method

02 · Root finding

False position method

Same bracket [a, b] as bisection, but instead of the midpoint, draw a straight line between (a, f(a)) and (b, f(b)) — the root of that line is the next guess.

03 · Root finding

Newton–Raphson method

f(x) = x² − 25, f′(x) = 2x. From a starting guess, follow the tangent line down to where it crosses zero — that's the next guess. Converges fast when it converges at all.

04 · Integration

Trapezoidal rule

f(x) = x² on [0, 2], split into 10 strips. Each strip is approximated as a trapezoid; the total area builds up one strip at a time.

Running sum
05 · Integration

Simpson's 1/3 rule

f(x) = x³ on [0, 1], split into 10 strips. Points alternate weight 4 and 2 (endpoints get weight 1) — a weighted sum that's exact for cubics.

Weighted sum
